Why the Red Planet still surprises scientists

Decades of orbiters and rovers have mapped Mars in remarkable detail, yet every new mission turns up something unexpected, from ancient river deltas to ice hiding just below the dusty surface.

Its two tiny moons, Phobos and Deimos, may be captured asteroids. Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the Solar System, rises nearly three times higher than Mount Everest, and the canyon system Valles Marineris would stretch across the entire United States.

Missions like Curiosity, Perseverance and the Ingenuity helicopter are searching for signs of ancient microbial life, while engineers plan how astronauts might one day live there.
